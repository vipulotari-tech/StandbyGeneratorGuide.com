# VOICE, SOUND & MUSIC — PRODUCTION SPEC
## Hindi VO (Phase B)
Record/generate each character as a **separate track**, Indian Hindi accents, natural spoken Hindi (no machine-translated phrasing). Measure actual spoken runtime after recording; target 1,800–2,000 words / ≥10:00 of meaningful content, ideal ≈13:30 with action beats. Re-time `subtitles/subtitles-hi.srt` to the real takes (current SRT follows the scene timing map). Emotion guide — Fear: short breaths, softer, urgent · Sadness: slower, restrained tremor · Anger: firm, never constant shouting · Mystery: slow, paused · Climax: controlled power. Lip-sync all dialogue close-ups in the edit; keep narration over non-speaking shots where possible.
## Music cue sheet (original/licensed only; never overpower dialogue)
S1 dark ambient synth + deep impact · S2 futuristic wonder, warm orchestra · S3 pulsing electronic tension · S4 emotional piano + restrained tension · S5 minimal piano, eerie silence, subtle distortion · S6 low brass, ominous strings · S7 dissonant psychological suspense · S8 rhythmic suspense percussion · S9 high-energy action · S10 countdown pulses, rising orchestra · S11 hopeful emotional orchestration · S12 powerful but restrained climax · S13 warm resolution → single mysterious electronic pulse → silence.
## SFX
Rain, thunder, distant traffic, sirens, footsteps, closing security doors, soft hospital monitors, ventilation, UI/digital interface sounds, radio comms. Use silence strategically (Scene 5 voice reveal, Scene 12 decision).
## Mix/master
Dialogue always intelligible; consistent sound perspective; no clipping/harsh jumps; ≈ **-14 LUFS integrated, peaks ≤ -1 dBTP**.
